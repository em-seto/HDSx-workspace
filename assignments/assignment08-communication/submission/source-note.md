
# Source Note

- Dataset: National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) classroom equity dataset (`examples/nhanes-equity/data/nhanes_equity_v6.csv`). Original survey source: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), National Center for Health Statistics (https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/).
- Evidence: Assignment 6, Table 1 (`assignments/assignment06-eda-ai-audit/submission/table1.csv`), generated using the analysis documented in `eda-note.qmd` in the same folder.
- The results are descriptive, unweighted estimates for adults aged 20–80. Missing income values were excluded from the grouped table, and missing BMI values were excluded from BMI calculations.

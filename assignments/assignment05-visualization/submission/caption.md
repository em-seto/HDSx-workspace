# Figure caption

Figure 1. Mean BMI by income group across nhanes survey cycles
- this figure shows the unweighted mean body mass index (BMI) for adults in different income groups across nhanes survey cycles, using the nhanes equity dataset
- Observations with missing BMI, income group, or survey cycle information were excluded
- The graph illustrates differences in mean BMI between income groups and changes across survey cycles
- The results are descriptive and don't establish a casual relationship betwen income and BMI
- The results shouldn't be interpreted as nationally representative estimates, because survey weights were not applied

# Limitation Note — Assignment 7

## Dashboard Limitations

The dashboard presents descriptive patterns in mean body mass index (BMI) across NHANES survey cycles among adults aged 20–80 within a selected income group.

Several limitations should be considered when interpreting these findings.

**1. Unweighted analysis**

The dashboard uses unweighted descriptive statistics and does not account for the complex NHANES survey design. Therefore, the results should not be interpreted as nationally representative estimates of BMI.

**2. Missing data and exclusions**

Observations with missing BMI or survey cycle information are excluded, along with individuals outside the specified age range or selected income group. These exclusions may affect the patterns observed.

**3. No causal interpretation**

Differences in mean BMI across survey cycles do not establish causal relationships between income and BMI. Other factors, such as demographic characteristics, health behaviours, and broader social conditions, may contribute to observed patterns.

**4. Classroom dataset**

The dashboard uses a prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom dataset rather than a full analysis of the original NHANES survey data. Its findings should be interpreted as an educational descriptive exercise.

## Appropriate Use

This dashboard can help public-health analysts explore descriptive BMI patterns, identify questions for further investigation, and communicate preliminary findings.

It should not be used to estimate national BMI trends, establish causal relationships, or make policy decisions without additional analysis and appropriate survey methods.

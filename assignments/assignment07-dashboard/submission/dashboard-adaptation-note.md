# Dashboard Adaptation Note — Assignment 7

## Intended Audience

The intended audience is public-health analysts preparing a short briefing on descriptive BMI patterns in the classroom NHANES equity dataset.

## Audience Question

Among adults aged 20–80, how does mean BMI vary across NHANES survey cycles for a selected income group?

## Adaptation Made

I revised the Interpretation section of the starter dashboard to make the findings more accessible and relevant to public-health analysts.

The original interpretation briefly described the dashboard's purpose. My revised version explains what the table and visualization show, how the descriptive patterns may support public-health discussions, and why the results should not be interpreted causally.

## Rationale

This adaptation improves knowledge translation by connecting the descriptive BMI patterns to the needs of the intended audience. It explains how analysts can use the dashboard to identify patterns worth investigating further without overstating what the data can establish.

The revised wording also reinforces the distinction between descriptive associations and causal conclusions.

## What Remained Unchanged

I did not modify the dataset, age restrictions, missing-data exclusions, income-group definitions, BMI calculations, or visualization code.

The dashboard continues to use the original descriptive, unweighted analysis from the starter file.

## Limitations

The dashboard uses an unweighted classroom dataset and does not account for the NHANES complex survey design. Therefore, the findings should not be interpreted as nationally representative estimates or evidence of a causal relationship between income and BMI.
